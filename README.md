# ASU4U 🌞💬

A campus-native support app for ASU students — part journal, part community, part peer connection — designed to make it easier to feel understood and less alone.

## The problem 🧠⚠️

A lot of students struggle quietly. Even when professional resources exist, many students don’t reach out, and when they do, it can feel intimidating, slow, or impersonal. At a university as large as ASU, the gap isn’t just “more counseling” — it’s day-to-day support, belonging, and real connection at scale.

Students already lean on peers first. The issue is that most peer support today is random, fragmented, and hard to find:
- You don’t know who will actually relate to what you’re going through
- Communities exist, but matching and follow-up are weak
- Asking for help publicly can feel unsafe or embarrassing
- It’s hard to discover what’s happening on campus and where community actually is

## Our solution 🚀🏫

ASU4U is a campus-native app built specifically for peer support at ASU. It’s not a generic social platform — it’s designed around privacy, context, and low-pressure connection.

### 1) Private daily check-in (mental calibration) 📝🔒
Every day starts with a quick, private “How are you feeling today?” check-in. This gives users a safe place to log what’s going on without needing to broadcast it.

### 2) A feed built for support (Pulse + Journal) 🧵📚
Home is split into two modes:
- Pulse: short, fast campus posts (vents, wins, questions, “anyone else?” moments)
- Journal: deeper reflections and stories for people who want to read/write more

Interactions are support-first:
- Relate, Support, Reply (public or private)
- “Most supportive reply” is highlighted so helpful responses rise to the top

### 3) People + Circles 🤝🫂
Users can find peers who actually match their context (major/year/interests/identity groups) and control privacy with Circles:
- Post to Campus or Circle
- Connect intentionally, not randomly

### 4) Groups that don’t feel dead 👥🌱
Groups are identity, academic, and interest communities that plug into the rest of the app — feed, matching, and events — so joining a group actually leads to participation.

### 5) Capsules (the “message that finds you” feature) 💌✨
Capsules are anonymous or identity-revealable messages students leave for other students on campus.

When someone completes their private daily check-in, ASU4U matches them to a capsule written for a moment like theirs (based on topic/tone). It’s a simple idea with a powerful effect: sometimes the right message at the right time is enough to help someone get through the day.

Users can:
- Read capsules anonymously
- Save them
- Send a thank-you
- Reveal identity only if the sender allows it

### 6) Events Map (campus-native discovery) 🗺️📍
ASU4U includes a simple campus Events Map:
- Major campus events
- Meet-and-greets
- Events from groups you follow
- Public meetups (study sessions, game watch parties, casual hangouts)

Tap an event to view details and join/interested.

## Why this matters at ASU 🌵🎓
ASU’s scale makes peer support essential for extending reach. ASU4U doesn’t replace professional services — it fills the everyday gap where students need belonging, reflection, and low-friction support.

## Key principles 🧭✅
- Campus-native (built around ASU life)
- Privacy-first (check-ins are private by default)
- Support-first interactions (not clout-driven)
- Connection with context (circles, groups, matching)
- Lightweight, real-time, and usable
